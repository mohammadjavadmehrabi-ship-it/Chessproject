# Privacy Policy — بازی مار (Snake Pro)

**Effective Date:** 2026-11-13
**App Name:** بازی مار (Snake Pro)
**Package Name:** `com.snakestudio.snake`

## Data Collection
**بازی مار (Snake Pro)** is a 100% offline game. We do **not** collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, location data, analytics, or telemetry.

## Permissions
This app requests **zero** Android permissions (no internet access, no storage access, no camera/microphone/contacts/location).

## Local Storage
Game preferences (selected game mode, speed, theme, language, sound mute setting) and local high scores are stored strictly on your device using Android `SharedPreferences`. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

## Children's Privacy
Because no personal information is collected, the app is safe for players of all ages.

## Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing page.
